NOBODYLOVESNONE

Until Someone Does

"A love letter never arrived to the main destination"

In the shadows of silent streets,
where whispers die before reaching ears,
hearts bleed in invisible ink,
and the moon cries over forgotten vows.

Every pulse is a confession
that no one will ever hear,
every sigh a promise
lost in the cold, endless night.

Love drifts like smoke through empty rooms,
touching nothing, leaving only the ache,
and yet, in the darkness,
a fragile hope clings to the faintest flame—
that maybe, somewhere, someone waits
to read what was never delivered.
